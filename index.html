COVID-19 Exploratory Data Analysis

A static snapshot of reported data for 225 countries and territories, analysed with Python, pandas, Matplotlib and Seaborn. By Saim Patel.

Most reported cases

The USA leads with 81.8M, followed by India with 43.0M.

Most reported deaths

USA 1.01M, Brazil 660K, India 521K.

Cases by continent / region

Europe has the highest total at 180.3M.

Highest death percentage

Yemen is highest at 18.15%. Small case counts can distort this measure.

Lowest case counts

Micronesia reported the fewest, with 1 case.

Population vs cases

Spearman correlation: 0.69.

Cases vs deaths
